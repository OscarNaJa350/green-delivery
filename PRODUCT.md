# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and school staff using a mobile-first dashboard to review carbon emissions and sustainability activity.

## Product Purpose

Oscar helps users understand daily carbon emissions, review contributing categories, and track related locations and activities.

## Brand Personality

Calm, encouraging, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense admin dashboards, alarmist climate messaging, and interfaces that make every item feel equally urgent.

## Design Principles

- Give each piece of information room to breathe.
- Make progress easy to understand at a glance.
- Encourage positive action with clear, friendly feedback.
- Keep the mobile experience comfortable for frequent use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use responsive layouts, readable text, clear interactive states, and support reduced motion where motion is added.
